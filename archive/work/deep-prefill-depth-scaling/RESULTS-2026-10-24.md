## Why this was run

Issue #214's reporter (RX 6700 XT 12 GB, gfx1031 as gfx1030, `-ncmoe 24`, q8_0 K/V) reports short-context
prefill much faster than upstream but long prompts slower (`pp512` +63 % at d0, +17 % at d32k, -6 % at d100k;
`read 117k` -19 %).  Their update confirmed the expert-cache arena never ran on their box, so the r123 band
clamp cannot be the explanation.  The constant-query/growing-depth sweep localises the shape to attention/KV
depth scaling.  This campaign checks whether it reproduces on gfx1201, whether it is our patch set or upstream
drift, and where the time goes.

## Root cause

`ggml_cuda_fattn_mma_get_config_rdna` (`ggml/src/ggml-cuda/fattn-mma-f16.cuh`) begins with two RDNA4
GQA-8/head-256 **band** rows keyed on `ncols` only.  The 64-column row returns
`fattn_mma_config(512, 1, 64, 64, 64, 32, 1, true)` (16 warps, occupancy 1, `nbatch_K2/V2` 64, combine 32).
The query-width dispatch in `fattn.cu` gives `ncols1 = 8, ncols2 = 8` (ncols 64) to **both** the W=5..8
verify band and every wide prefill (`Q->ne[1] > 4`), and the row feeds `__launch_bounds__`, so it is compiled
into the instantiation that prefill uses.  Upstream's row for `(256,256,64)` is `(256, 2, 64, 128, 128, 64, 1,
true)`.

## Prototype

One-line row swap (upstream's row) in `~/llama.cpp`, rebuilt with `~/bin/build-llama-rocm`:
**+36 % at d98304, +36 % at d65536, +19 % at d32768, +3 % at d0**, and 20 % above upstream master at d98304.

The swap alone is not the fix: the row also gives the W=5..8 band its geometry (the source comment measures
~17 % for q8_0 at that band), so the fix must key the band row on a compile-time band flag (a distinct
instantiation) and leave prefill on the 256-thread row.  That touches prefill FA's KV-row split/combine order,
so it owes `scripts/gate-prefill-logits.sh` plus the band's own decode/verify A/B.

The working tree was restored to the delivery after the prototype and rebuilt (`RESTORE_EXIT=0`); no
delivery file was changed by this campaign.
